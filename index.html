The synergy constant is an empirical coefficient from Yamaguchi's trained-panel taste experiments (Ajinomoto Central Research Labs; Yamaguchi 1967, J. Food Science 32:473). It is fitted, not derived, and only valid with concentrations in g/100 ml over roughly 0.01–0.5 g/100 ml. That is why only the per-bowl figure is shown here.
